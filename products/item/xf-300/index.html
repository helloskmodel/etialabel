XF-300 — 5 mil Flame-Retardant Woven-Nylon Wire-Marking Label

Request a SampleRequest a Quote
Overview

Overview

POLYONICS XF-300 is a flame-retardant white woven nylon label for wire, cable and harness marking, built on a conformable self-laminating facestock with a high-temperature acrylic adhesive and designed for thermal-transfer printing.

Features

Features

  • Flexible white woven-nylon facestock, conformable for wire and cable wrap
  • Self-laminating wrap-around marking
  • High-temperature acrylic adhesive
  • Thermal-transfer printable
  • Flame-retardant — UL94 VTM-0 rated
