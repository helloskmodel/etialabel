Industry Overview

Industry Overview

Every automotive part needs clear, reliable identification. ETIA labels deliver strong adhesion and stable performance under heat, chemicals, moisture and the daily challenges of vehicle life.

From under the hood to the door jam — engine bay, batteries, wire harnesses, VIN plates, tires, fuel and EV charging ports — our labels stay legible and firmly in place, and look great for years to come.

Strong

Multi-layer construction for stable performance and a long service life.

Smooth

Easy application with excellent print quality and reliable adhesion.

Versatile

Bonds to many surfaces — metal, plastic, painted parts and glass.
